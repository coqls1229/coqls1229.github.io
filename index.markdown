## About

<div class="about-body" markdown="1">

I am a M.S. in Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by professor Jongchul Ye in the <a class="bispl-link" href="https://bispl-website.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>BISPL</strong></a>. \\

Through my work, I aim to uncover meaningful patterns in complex video data and develop novel 3D visual models. \\
I am passionate about applying AI research to solve real-world problems and creating meaningful impact, while collaborating with others to push the boundaries of video and 3D vision technologies.
